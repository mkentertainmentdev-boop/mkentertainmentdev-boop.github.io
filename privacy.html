Pulley Puzzle

Privacy Policy · Effective date: 17 October 2026
Developer: MK Entertainment (Mohammad Kasim)

1. Overview

Pulley Puzzle ("the game") is a physics puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used. The game does not require an account, and the developer does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, settings such as language, music and sound) is saved only on your own device. This data is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game removes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)
  • Interactions with ads and basic app usage and diagnostic data

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Internet connection check

When you choose to watch a reward ad, the game may make a simple request to a Google server to check whether your device is online. No personal information is sent with this request.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and data deletion

Because the developer does not collect personal data, there is nothing stored with the developer to delete. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. For data collected by Google for advertising, use your Android ad settings or Google's tools described above.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: mkentertainment.dev@gmail.com
